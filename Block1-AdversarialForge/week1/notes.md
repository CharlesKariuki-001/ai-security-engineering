# Notes - Tensors Basics

- Tensors are just multi-dimensional arrays (numbers in grids).
- Scalars (0D), Vectors (1D), Matrices (2D), 3D+ tensors.
- Matrix multiplication is the core operation in all neural networks.
- Gradients tell us how to change inputs to change the output — this is how both training and attacks work.

Learned: The "intelligence" in AI is mostly matrix multiplications + gradients.
